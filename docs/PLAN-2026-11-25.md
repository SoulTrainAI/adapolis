## 5. Risks

**Legal (Hawaii gambling = prize + chance + consideration; AG Opinion 16-1; all items for the Gate 4 lawyer review)**
- **R1 — Pay-to-win and "entry requirement" language.** The NFT discount (Q1) makes buying an NFT improve your
  odds at NFT prizes: the clearest "consideration" in the design, worse than the Collector's Cup edition
  already flagged. Line 9's wording frames 100 ADA as the price of playing. Both push the game toward a paid
  contest.
- **R2 — Removing on-page disclaimers.** Fine for UX if the Terms carry the no-betting and collectibles-only
  policy and the mint screen keeps one line. Removing them everywhere *and* adding marketplace links (WayUp) and
  a royalty to ADApolis makes the "collectible, not investment" framing rest on less.
- **R3 — Team test pressings for "business purposes".** If ADApolis sells them, ADApolis is a primary seller of
  the NFTs it awards. Disclose in Terms and NFT docs (planned) and keep them visibly marked.
- **R4 — Terms/Privacy written by me** are drafts. Have the same lawyer review them.
- **R5 — Email addresses** are personal data: needs the Privacy Policy live first, unsubscribe in every email,
  and Supabase RLS that only the service role can read them.

**Cost**
- Hourly sync: more Koios calls (free tier has limits; watch for 429s). Haiku quote extraction: cents per
  proposal, cached. Email provider: free tiers exist, paid beyond. unavatar free tier: already cached server-side.
  Nothing here needs money without asking you first.

**Cardinal Rule**
- Commentary now shows vote direction. Guard: vote direction is never an input to points, projections, Draft
  Cost or matching-to-scoring; a unit test enforces it.
- Draft Cost from Projected Points is process-based ✅. Forum/Discord points (L3) are process-based but
  farmable; Season 2 at the earliest, with caps.

**Trust and product**
- Admin edits to DRep-signed profiles (Q3) are the biggest trust risk in the list.
- Hot Takes quotes must be verbatim and in context; the verification step drops any quote that isn't an exact
  match, and each links to its source.
- Mobile deep-link formats differ by wallet and change; each is tested on a real link before shipping, with the
  in-wallet-browser route as the fallback that always works.
- Salary cap + underdog multiplier both favour small DReps (they're usually cheap *and* boosted). Probably
  fine, but I'll check the Preseason projections to make sure a 5-small-DRep team isn't an obvious dominant
  strategy, and report back before Season 1.
